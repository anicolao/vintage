# Product Vision

## Vision

Vintage gives every Vinted seller an expert listing and pricing partner.

A seller should be able to photograph an item, add a few words when useful, and receive a polished listing proposal that sounds like them, represents the item accurately, and positions it to earn the best return. The seller brings the item and their judgment; Vintage brings attentive visual analysis, knowledge of their selling style, and market-aware pricing intelligence.

## Why this should exist

The quality of a marketplace listing has a direct effect on buyer confidence, discoverability, and sale value. Creating that listing requires the seller to inspect the item, identify its attributes, describe it clearly, compare it with the market, and decide how to price it. Repeating that work for every item is slow, and pricing decisions are especially vulnerable to weak comparisons and low-price anchoring.

Vintage turns that expertise into an accessible, repeatable experience. It helps sellers express the value of each item clearly and make pricing decisions based on the full opportunity in the market.

## Who we serve

Vintage serves Vinted sellers who value their time and want to earn a strong return from the items they sell. This includes occasional wardrobe clear-outs, active second-hand enthusiasts, and high-volume sellers. Each seller gets an experience shaped by their own catalogue, language, standards, and commercial priorities.

## The product promise

Give Vintage photos of an item and it will propose a listing worth publishing.

That proposal combines three kinds of intelligence:

### Visual understanding

Vintage looks closely at every photo to identify the item, its distinguishing details, its condition, and the attributes buyers care about. It uses those observations to create a complete, specific draft and highlights details that deserve the seller's attention during review.

### Seller understanding

Vintage learns from the seller's existing listings. It recognizes how they write titles, organize descriptions, communicate condition, describe fit, use formatting, and present their items. Each new proposal preserves that recognizable approach so the seller's catalogue remains coherent and personal.

### Pricing intelligence

Vintage evaluates the item's market position and recommends a price that maximizes expected seller revenue. It weighs comparable items by relevance, examines the broader price distribution, recognizes attributes that support a premium, and accounts for demand, time to sale, and negotiation dynamics.

The result is a recommended listing price, an expected sale range, and a clear explanation of the opportunity and tradeoffs.

## The experience we want

Listing an item should feel like showing it to a capable partner.

The seller uploads photos and can add a short note. Vintage returns a draft in the seller's style, a structured interpretation of the item, and a pricing recommendation grounded in relevant evidence. The seller can see what the AI observed, where it is uncertain, why it chose the price, and how the recommendation changes with different sale-speed preferences.

The seller reviews and shapes the proposal, then approves it with confidence. Their edits become useful signals for understanding their preferences and improving future proposals.

## Product principles

### Start with the photos

The default experience asks very little of the seller. Careful image understanding does the initial work, and concise prompts request seller input where it adds real value.

### Sound like the seller

Personalization comes from the seller's demonstrated choices across their existing listings. Generated copy should feel consistent with their voice while improving clarity and completeness.

### Price for value

Pricing starts with the specific item's revenue opportunity. Vintage weights comparable listings by relevance and quality, recognizes evidence that supports premium positioning, and evaluates the full price distribution. Recommendations account for differentiation, demand, negotiation, and the relationship between price and probability of sale.

### Make reasoning inspectable

Sellers can understand which visual details, listing patterns, comparables, and market signals shaped a proposal. Uncertainty is surfaced in the review experience so judgment is focused where it matters.

### Learn through approval

Seller edits and approvals teach Vintage how that person describes items, evaluates condition, and approaches pricing. The experience becomes more tailored and efficient with continued use.

### Keep the seller decisive

Vintage provides a strong first proposal and clear evidence. The seller applies their knowledge, makes edits, chooses the price, and gives final approval.

## What makes Vintage valuable

Vintage brings together capabilities that are usually fragmented across manual research, generic writing tools, marketplace search, and the seller's memory:

- multimodal understanding of the actual item;
- personalization from the seller's full listing history;
- listing copy and structured attributes generated together;
- pricing optimized for expected revenue;
- evidence that distinguishes strong comparables from superficial matches; and
- a review loop that improves the assistant for each seller.

## How we define success

Vintage succeeds when sellers can move from photos to an approved listing in minutes, recognize themselves in the result, trust the description of the item, understand the pricing recommendation, and realize more value from what they sell.

The lasting advantage is a deep understanding of three things at once: the item in front of the camera, the individual seller behind it, and the market into which it will be listed.
